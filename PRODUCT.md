# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Algerian consumers browsing a COD e-commerce store on desktop and mobile. They're price-conscious, value trust and familiarity, and shop with intent - searching for specific products or browsing categories. The primary context is a home screen or landing experience that should feel like a destination, not just a utility.

## Product Purpose

A COD (Cash on Delivery) e-commerce platform for Algeria that connects shoppers with products they need. The catalog page serves as the front door - the first and most-visited surface. It needs to build trust, communicate energy and local relevance, and make browsing feel like discovery rather than database queries.

## Brand Personality

Bold, Energetic, Confident. This should feel like a streetwear drop or a limited release - product-forward with typographic swagger. Not cute, not minimal, not safe. Think urban boutique energy: graphic, dynamic, self-assured.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS e-commerce templates. No cookie-cutter "Shopify default" look.
- No sterile marketplace density (Amazon/Shein clutter).
- No over-designed startup aesthetic with gradient blobs and glassmorphism.
- No hero-metric template (big number, small label, gradient accent).
- No side-stripe borders or gradient text decorations.

## Design Principles

1. **Typography as texture.** Let type carry the energy. Bold weights, generous scale, dynamic rhythm.
2. **Product is hero.** Products aren't thumbnails in a grid - they're the star of every section. Big images, space to breathe.
3. **Rhythm over grid.** Vary spacing for drama. Tight clusters followed by breathing room. Not every section needs the same padding.
4. **Confident dark.** Lean into dark mode as the primary aesthetic. Light as a clean alternative, but the soul is high-contrast and nocturnal.
5. **Local, not generic.** Avoid global marketplace tropes. This is an Algerian store with character, not a white-label template.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard best practices: good color contrast, keyboard navigation, focus indicators, reduced motion support via prefers-reduced-motion. No strict WCAG compliance target but aim for AA where practical.
